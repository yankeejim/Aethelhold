# Aethelhold: resources

Agreed with Jim, 2026-10-18. Resources work as in Songs of Syx and RimWorld: raw materials
from the land, workshops that process them, and goods the city uses.

## Raw (gathered from the map)

- **Land:** wood, stone, clay, sand.
- **Wild food and animals:** berries and foraged plants, game meat, fish, hides.
- **Farming:** grain, vegetables, herbs.
- **Livestock:** wool, milk, eggs.
- **Mining:** copper, tin, iron ore, coal.

## Processed (made in workshops)

- **Building:** planks, cut stone, bricks, lime, glass.
- **Fuel:** firewood, charcoal.
- **Food:** flour, then bread; cooked meals; beer and ale.
- **Goods:** leather, cloth, clothes, pottery, furniture.
- **Metal:** copper, bronze, iron, steel, then tools, weapons and armor.

## Rules

- **Physical items.** Every log, stone and fish exists in the world. It lies where it was
  gathered or made until someone carries it to a stockpile or workshop. Hauling is real work.
- **Spoilage.** Food rots over time unless it is stored, smoked, dried or kept cold. Cold
  weather slows rot. Running out of good food in winter is the main threat of the founding.
- **Skills.** People get better at the work they do. A skilled worker works faster and wastes
  less, and passes skill on to their students and to the workers under them. A master who dies
  untaught takes their skill with them.
- **Quality (later, not in the founding).** Skilled workers make better goods (poor, normal,
  fine and up).
- **Fantasy materials (later).** Monster parts (scales, venom, dragon blood) and rare
  materials come once magic is designed; they tie in with expeditions.

## The founding uses only

Wood, firewood, stone, berries, meat, fish, hides and simple tools: enough for food, warmth
and shelter through the first winter.
