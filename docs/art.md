# Aethelhold: art

Agreed with Jim, 2026-10-18: the look is Manor Lords (grounded, natural, detailed). Free art
first; buy a few key packs (characters, buildings) once the look is proven.

## Who makes what

- **Code (Claude):** terrain, lighting (Lumen, Nanite, sky, clouds, fog, weather), materials
  that blend ground textures, scattering forests and plants (PCG), the camera, and placing
  everything in the world. All of it is generated by scripts in `Unreal/Tools/`, never by hand.
- **Fab (Jim downloads, with his Epic sign-in):** scanned ground, rock, plant and tree
  assets, characters, animation, and medieval buildings.

## Where downloaded art goes

Fab adds packs to `Unreal/Content/Fab` (or `Megascans`). Those folders are not stored in git
(they are large and can be downloaded again), so keep a list here of every pack in use.

## What we need, in order

1. **Ground:** grass, forest floor, dirt or mud, river stones, rock cliff (tiling materials).
2. **Nature:** a European forest set (oak, beech, pine, birch), bushes, grass and flower
   clumps, rocks and boulders, fallen logs.
3. **Animation:** Epic's free Game Animation Sample (walking, running, idles).
4. **People:** medieval peasant characters (or MetaHumans, free with Unreal, dressed in period
   clothes).
5. **Buildings:** a medieval village kit with timber, thatch and stone, in pieces so buildings
   can rise in stages.
